I have a weird relationship with my neighbor’s cat, I think.  
I don’t know if I should pet her. What if she doesn’t want to be petted?  
So I wait instead – for her to reach out to me.  
She reaches for me by rubbing against my feet.  
It is a sign, I think. So I pet her.

She lies on her side, asking for more, I think. I pet her more.  
So with time, I get cocky.  
I begin tapping at her feet. She tries to bite me. So I stop.  
I know how to annoy her, I think.  
Sometimes, I do like annoying her.  
She's become comfortable with me, I think.  
She only pretends to be annoyed at me, I think.  
I think about her when I'm away from her.  
Did she eat her food?  
She likes looking out my window, I think.  
I wonder what she thinks.

I have a strange relationship with my parents’ dog, I think.  
He looks at me and runs to the cabinet.  
He is showing me where they keep his food, I think.  
He barks at me.  
He is telling me that he wants food, I think.  
When my parents aren't home, he sleeps by my side.  
I wonder if he has a hierarchical system.  
I wonder where he thinks he falls in his hierarchical system.  
I wonder if he looks up or looks down.  
He pretends to bite me when I'm working on my laptop.  
He wants my attention, I think.

I watched my friend's dogs.  
They take turns being naughty and shy.  
He is shy at home. She is shy outside.  
They like being close to one another, I think.  
I wonder if they can talk to each other.

I think I’m in a state of search — always have been.
Maybe because my mother, growing up, told me to search for three stars in the sky.
But I think it’s rather hard to find three stars in Mumbai skies.
Some of them turn out to be planes. 
Some of them turn out to be faint light from a distant building. A smudge of gray on the building.

In a world of what-ifs, it’s the confirmation of luck, she said. 
I don’t believe that finding three stars in the sky would make one lucky.
I don’t know how one knows what good fortune is.
Anyhow — the habit stuck, I suppose. 

So I’ve searched for three stars in every sky I stumble upon. 

Three years back, I sat on the steps outside my apartment in hope…looking at the night.
In supposition, I did find three stars. 
I find it rather strange that, growing up, I heard Venus was the brightest star in the sky. 

And every birthday, I receive at least one letter or a postcard that says “don’t change”.
I wear a necklace. My friend gifted it to me on my birthday three years ago. It’s a Libra constellation.
It’s strange that Libra is the only zodiac constellation that is an inanimate object. 

I wanted to be an astronomer and a photographer growing up. 
Maybe because I wear spectacles. 
Or maybe I’m intrigued by the invisible in different spectra.
Now, I reach for astronomy to explain life. Thus I joke and say I’m an astrologer in the making.

When I was in the seventh grade, I learnt that looking at the night sky is like looking into the past. 
Maybe the star is already dead before it reaches my eye.

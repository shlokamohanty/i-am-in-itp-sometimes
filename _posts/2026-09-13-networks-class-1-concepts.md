> A **network** is a collection of things connected to each other. Put more formally, networks are usually described as a series of _nodes_ connected by _links_.

> A node can be anything or anyone that can communicate with other nodes over the links between them.

> The links that connect the nodes in a network are the medium of communication, and they affect how we communicate in a network.

> **Centralized networks** are networks in which all nodes are linked to one central node, and all communication flows through the center. In a diagram, they look like a star.

The way my Obsidian is set up for my personal blog, it's almost as though there are clusters of centralized networks.

> **Decentralized networks** are collections of  several hubs of nodes. Each hub has a central node, but the hubs are all linked together through other links.

> The most connected network would be one in which every node is connected directly to every other node.

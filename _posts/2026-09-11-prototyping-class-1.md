### solder - tube
- flux core solder automatically applies flux
- if temperature is high then flux will evaporate
- solder station with fine temperature control

solder temperature - (600°F - 700°F)

```
if solder is cold:
  won't melt solder
elif solder is hot:
  damage components
  burn off flux
  reduce lifespan of tip
```

- avoid putting it directly on joint unless necessary 
- solder on solder joint
- apply fresh solder on tip before turning it off

**clean the tip**
- solder on tip to protect it
- brass
- little water on sponge then clean
- paper towel and make it slightly wet if no cleaning supply is available

**choosing a solder tip?**
- fine
- pointed for something small
- screwdriver for something big

```
if leaded solder:
  won't melt solder
elif lead-free solder:
  damage components
  burn off flux
  reduce lifespan of tip
```

**avoid bad soldering practices**
- apply flux
- touch pad
- apply solder wire
- remove solder wire and iron

solder on the copper part
add flux if it doesn't flow freely

**pcbs**
green -> best to absorb UV

connect wires by twisting them
stronger connection by twisting it -> heat shrink

**good bread-boarding practices**
- tidier wires
- 22 AWG wire
- wire wrapping -> for making inductors?
